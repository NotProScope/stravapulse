Your running community, on one map.

Meets, open tracks, club runs and track workouts near you, sorted by the day and by your pace.

  1. Open Maps and answer the two personalize questions
  2. Plan my first week, then tap Add all
  3. Tap the SMC track pin to see the live lanes and the workout board
  4. Open Westside Mile Night to see the prediction, events and heat sheet
  5. Fast-forward to race day, then open What's next
